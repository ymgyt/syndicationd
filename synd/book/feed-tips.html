The crates.io feed announcement also lists registry-wide feeds for new crates and new versions.

Sites without a feed

For a blog without its own feed, look it up in Olshansk/rss-feeds. Copy the URL from the Feed column into your reader, for example the Anthropic Engineering feed.

To look up a page on Open RSS, insert openrss.org/ after https:// in its URL and open that address in your browser. If a feed is available, copy the subscription URL from the preview. For example, the r/HelixEditor preview links to an Atom feed for hot posts. Reddit retired its native .rss feeds in November 2026.
